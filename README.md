## 🎨 Creating Your First Macro Pad

Once you've launched VRMacroPad, you'll see a blank canvas ready for your buttons. Here's how to make your first one:

1. **Add a Button**: Look for a "+" or "Add" button and click it.
2. **Choose an Action**: Select what you want the button to do. You might pick "Switch OBS Scene," "Toggle Voicemeeter Mute," or "Send Twitch Message."
3. **Customize the Look**: Pick a color, add a label, and choose an icon if you like. Make it yours.
4. **Place It**: In VR, grab it and put it where you want. On desktop, just drag it around the window.

You can make as many buttons as you need. Organize them into groups, resize them, and arrange them however feels natural to you.
